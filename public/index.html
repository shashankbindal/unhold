Audit & Settings

Every action, the exact payload hash, and how this environment is wired.

  • Code decides. Risk scores, amounts and fixes are computed by deterministic rules. The model only reads them.
  • AI explains. Explanations, root causes and buyer drafts are labelled as AI inference and quote your own data verbatim.
  • You approve. Any action that moves money (refunds) waits for your explicit approval.
  • Hash-bound approvals. Each approval is bound to a sha256 of the exact {order, capture, amount} payload; a changed payload cannot be executed.
  • Low-risk writes only. Without approval the agent can only add tracking numbers that already exist in your shipping export.
  • Full audit trail. Every assessment, tool call and PayPal write is logged with its payload hash.
  • Cannot release holds. Unhold does not know or influence PayPal's internal risk decisions.

Unhold infers likely hold risk from your own order, refund and dispute data using a transparent heuristic. It does not know or influence PayPal's internal risk decisions. Holds in this demo are simulated because the PayPal sandbox does not withhold funds. Every action that moves money needs your approval.
